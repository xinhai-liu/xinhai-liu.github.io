Profile

Technology, credit and institutions

Trained in engineering and data science, I spent seven years at the Credit Reference Center of the People’s Bank of China before expanding my work into AI, financial technology, data governance and international credit research.

My current agenda asks a simple question: how can better information infrastructure and intelligent systems make financial markets more effective, resilient and trustworthy?
