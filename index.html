About

Between research, regulation and real-world financial systems

I work on the infrastructure that sits behind modern credit markets: how data is collected, interpreted and governed; how financial institutions assess risk; and how new technologies can improve both efficiency and consumer outcomes.

My background spans artificial intelligence, credit reporting and financial policy. I received my PhD from KU Leuven and spent years working on credit information and risk-related research in China. Today, I focus increasingly on the convergence of AI, credit technology and the personal data economy.

I am especially interested in ideas that can travel across borders: what China, Europe, Korea, the United States and other markets can learn from one another about credit infrastructure, data governance and financial innovation.

Research

Core research agenda

A portfolio of long-horizon questions with practical implications for banks, regulators, fintechs and consumers.

AI & Credit Technology

How large models, agents and machine intelligence can improve underwriting, post-loan management, credit analysis, customer service and risk governance.

Extending firm-level credit analysis toward connected risk: ownership, guarantees, supply chains, network exposure and systemic transmission.

D

Data portability, trusted sharing, personal agents, consent architecture and new business models built around user-controlled data.

E

Cross-Border Credit & Data Governance

Credit intelligence for international trade, overseas expansion, SME finance, compliance and cross-border data flows.

F

Credit Education & Consumer Protection

Practical frameworks that help consumers understand credit, navigate debt problems and rebuild financial resilience.

Global engagement

A cross-border perspective on finance, data and technology

My work is grounded in international comparison. I engage with researchers, financial institutions, credit bureaus, technology companies and professional networks across Asia, Europe and other markets.

Areas of particular interest include Korean credit and MyData infrastructure, European data governance, U.S. consumer reporting, international credit services and new institutional models for AI-enabled finance.

I welcome collaboration on research, policy dialogue, executive education, cross-border projects and applied experiments with financial institutions.
