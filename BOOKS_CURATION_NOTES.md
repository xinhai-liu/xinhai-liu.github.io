# Books Curation Notes — V2.2

## Editorial logic

The Books page is curated for an international audience rather than reproducing the CV chronologically.

It is organized into four narratives:

1. **Current work (2026)** — consumer credit repair, MyData and the personal data economy.
2. **Foundational books** — credit reporting, big data and FinTech.
3. **Reference, translation & edited work** — works that connect Chinese readers with international credit and FinTech knowledge.
4. **Longer-form research** — the KU Leuven PhD thesis as the technical foundation.

## Important title note

For Chinese-language books, the English titles on the website are descriptive translations unless an official English title is explicitly present in the CV. The original Chinese title is retained directly below the English rendering so it is easy to correct later.
