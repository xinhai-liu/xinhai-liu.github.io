Research theme

AI & Credit Technology

How intelligent systems are changing credit analysis, underwriting, post-loan management and financial decision-making.

Why it matters

The central question

AI is moving from isolated prediction models toward systems that can interpret, reason, retrieve evidence and coordinate actions. Credit markets are a natural testing ground because they combine high-value decisions, rich data and strict governance requirements.
