Research theme

MyData & Personal Data Economy

What happens when individuals gain greater control over the data that describes them?

Why it matters

The central question

MyData, data portability and personal AI shift the role of individuals from passive data subjects toward more active participants in data markets. The central policy and business question is how to combine user control, trusted sharing and economically useful data flows.
