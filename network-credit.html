Research theme

6C & Network Credit

From the risk of one company to the risk of connected groups and networks.

Why it matters

The central question

Traditional credit analysis is largely firm-centric. Yet modern firms are embedded in ownership structures, guarantees, supply chains, financing relationships and transaction networks. “Connection” can therefore be treated as a distinct analytical dimension rather than a footnote to conventional 5C frameworks.
