# Publications — First-Round Curation Notes

This first round turns the CV into a curated international-facing publication portfolio rather than a complete bibliography.

## Selection logic

The page deliberately balances four dimensions:

1. **Technical foundations** — IEEE TKDE, IEEE TPAMI, JASIST and empirical credit-scoring work.
2. **6C / network credit lineage** — guarantee networks, contagion paths and enterprise connections.
3. **MyData / personal data economy** — Korea, data portability and personal-data business models.
4. **Consumer credit & policy** — credit reporting, credit repair, alternative scoring and financial inclusion.

## Items to add later

- DOI / publisher links for papers where the CV does not provide a URL.
- Google Scholar and ORCID links on the Contact page.
- Optional “Complete Bibliography” PDF or CV link.
- Additional Chinese-language professional articles only when they strengthen one of the four core themes.

## Editing

In `publications.html`, every group is marked with `EDIT HERE` comments. Duplicate or remove one full `<article class="list-row">...</article>` block at a time.
