Research theme

Cross-Border Credit

Building information infrastructure for firms and individuals operating across borders.

Why it matters

The central question

International expansion creates an information problem: banks, insurers, buyers, platforms and regulators often lack a common way to assess counterparties across jurisdictions. Cross-border credit research asks how credit intelligence, compliance and trusted data exchange can reduce that friction.
